Restoring binary division

Divide ten by three in binary and see where each quotient bit comes from.

Divide 10 by 3 one quotient bit at a time

In 1010 ÷ 0011, bring down each dividend bit. If you can subtract 3, write quotient bit 1 and keep the difference. Otherwise write 0 and keep the partial remainder. A restoring divider tries the subtraction and adds 3 back when it goes negative.

The quotient is 0011 (3), and the remainder is 1. Check 3 × 3 + 1 = 10. A zero divisor is invalid.
